{%- comment -%} Produces a plain-text summary for a post. Prefers an explicit `description`, falls back to the Liquid excerpt, and finally to the body — older posts open with a centred image, which leaves the excerpt empty once tags are stripped. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign summary = include.post.description -%} {%- unless summary and summary != '' -%} {%- assign summary = include.post.excerpt | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | strip -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- if summary == '' -%} {%- assign summary = include.post.content | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | strip -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign limit = include.length | default: 170 -%} {{- summary | truncate: limit -}}
